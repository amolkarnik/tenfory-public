# Tenfory

Tenfory reads the PDF of your Form 1040 and your W-2s and 1099s on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, then shows how your income, taxes and retirement savings changed from year to year.

There is no account and no server. Your returns are read on your device and saved only in your own iCloud Drive or on the device.

- **Import** your filed Form 1040 (tax years 2020–2025), W-2, 1099-INT, 1099-DIV, 1099-G, 1099-B and 1098 PDFs.
- **Read or entered:** every figure comes from your documents or from you, and each return is checked against the form's own arithmetic.
- **See the years together:** income mix, taxes withheld, effective rate, 401(k) contributions and savings rate, or one year at a time.
- **Try it with sample data** for a made-up household before importing your own.
