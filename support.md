# Support

For questions, bug reports or feature requests, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/amolkarnik/tenfory-public/issues).

**Never attach a real tax return, W-2 or 1099, or a screenshot showing your figures, names or numbers.** Describe the problem instead (which form, which tax year, which line or box), or reproduce it with the app's sample data.

Please include:
- Device and system version (for example iPhone, iOS 26; Mac, macOS 26)
- App version (Settings)
- The steps that lead to the problem, what you expected and what happened

## Frequently asked questions

### Which documents can Tenfory read?
The PDF of a filed Form 1040 for tax years 2020 through 2025 (with Schedule 1), and W-2, 1099-INT, 1099-DIV, 1099-G, 1099-B and 1098 forms. The Form 1040 PDF must have selectable text, such as the PDF saved by tax software; photos or scans of a paper Form 1040 are not read yet. Individual W-2s and 1099s can be scans. Form 1040-SR and amended returns (1040-X) are not supported.

### Why does a figure say "Missing"?
Tenfory never estimates. Gross income, for example, needs your pre-tax 401(k) contributions, which come from W-2 box 12 code D. Import your W-2 PDFs for that year, or tap the button next to the missing figure to enter it.

### What does "Needs review" mean?
A line on your return didn't match the form's arithmetic, or could only be read by its position on the page. Open the item in Documents to see which line to compare with your paper form, then correct it or tap "Mark as checked".

### Where is my data stored?
In the app's folder in your iCloud Drive, or on the device if iCloud Drive is off. Nothing is sent to the developer. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

### How do I move my data to a new device?
Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on; the data appears automatically. You can also use Settings → "Export backup (JSON)".

### How do I delete everything?
Settings → "Reset everything". You can also delete the app's folder in iCloud Drive.
